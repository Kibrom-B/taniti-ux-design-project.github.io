FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers to common questions.

ALCOHOL & LOCAL LAWS

What is the drinking age in Taniti?

The drinking age is 18.


When can alcohol be sold or served?

Alcohol cannot be sold or served from midnight to 9:00 a.m.

SAFETY AND HEALTH

What should visitors know about safety?

Violent crime is very rare, but petty crime and pickpocket have increased with tourism.


Is medical care available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics.

ABOUT TANITI

What currency is used?

The U.S. dollar is used on Taniti. Some businesses also accept euros and yen.


Is English spoken?

Many younger Tanitians speak English. English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents.


Are credit cards accepted?

Many businesses accept major credit cards.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Are attractions open on holidays?

Some attractions and restaurants may close on national holidays.


What should I know about transportation?

Taniti offers buses, taxis, rental cars, bicycles, and other transportation options
